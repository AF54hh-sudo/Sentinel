# Enterprise Discount Governance Policy

## Approval principles and monitoring requirements

AMX Tech permits Enterprise discounts when the commercial rationale is documented and the proposed terms remain within the delegated approval band. Acceptable rationales include multi-team adoption, competitive replacement, longer commitment, or a strategic renewal with a credible expansion plan. Discounts are not intended to conceal weak product fit or to substitute for unresolved service issues. The account owner must record gross value, proposed discount, expected net value, seats, term, and approval owner before the agreement is finalized.

Exception approvals require review by Sales Operations and Finance. Reviewers consider the size of the concession, customer history, expected expansion, renewal timing, and consistency with comparable agreements. No approval guarantees that a deal will be profitable because shared infrastructure and support costs are not allocated reliably at customer level. The policy therefore governs commercial discipline rather than providing a customer-level contribution model.

Monitoring should compare average and distributional discount rates by plan, region, channel, and period. Analysts must preserve the accounting identity between gross revenue, discount amount, and net revenue. A wider gross-to-net gap should be investigated alongside changes in deal mix and customer counts. The policy cautions against judging an individual negotiation solely from an aggregate average, and against claiming that discounting caused later retention without an appropriate design.

Quarterly review owners are responsible for identifying repeated exceptions, missing rationale, or approval concentration. When management asks whether discounting affected Q2 performance, this policy explains why Enterprise agreements are a relevant secondary factor. It does not provide the measured change or establish its relative importance versus infrastructure cost. Those conclusions require the governed sales and cost evidence.
